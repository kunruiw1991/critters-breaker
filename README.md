# 🌈🧱🐾 Critter Rainbow Brick Breaker! (Smiling Critters)

🎮 **Play Live (GitHub Pages):** **[https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-breaker/](https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-breaker/)**

A **100% Zero-Text**, colorful physics **Rainbow Brick Breaker & Critter Rescue** game starring all **8 Smiling Critters** (**DogDay**, **CatNap**, **CraftyCorn**, **Hoppy Hopscotch**, **PickyPiggy**, **Bubba Bubbaphant**, **Bobby BearHug**, and **KickinChicken**)!

---

## ✨ Features & Calibrated "Goldilocks" Gameplay

- **100% Visual Zero-Text Interface**:
  - Designed for young children with **zero text** anywhere in the UI, HUD, stage selector, buttons, or celebration modals.
  - Guided by Smiling Critter portraits, glowing target bubble badges, 3 star slots (`⭐`), and an animated finger hint (`👆`).
- **Rainbow Cloud Trampoline & Aim Line**:
  - Slide your Smiling Critter's bouncy **Rainbow Cloud Trampoline** left and right (`👆` touch/mouse drag or `⬅️`/`➡️` arrow keys).
  - Aim with the swinging dotted sightline and tap anywhere (or press `🚀` / `Space`) to launch!
- **Rescue Trapped Smiling Critters (`🫧`) & Smash Star Bricks (`⭐`)**:
  - Bounce rainbow balls into the brick mosaic to shatter candy bricks, crack `2-HP` armored crystal bricks (`🛡️`), collect all **3 Giant Star Bricks (`⭐`)**, and pop open the glowing **Critter Bubble Cages**!
  - Clearing all bricks around a Critter Bubble Cage or hitting the cage directly frees the Critter in a shower of hearts (`💖`)!
- **Falling Gift Capsules (`🎁`) & Left Dock Power-Ups**:
  - `🚀` **Launch & Twin Star Blasters**: Launches the ball and fires twin upward star lasers from the Critter's trampoline!
  - `⚽` **Multi-Ball (`×3`)**: Splits active balls into 3 rainbow energy balls for chain-reaction brick smashing!
  - `☄️` **Fireball Comet**: Ignites balls into blazing rainbow comets that plow straight through bricks and cages without bouncing back!
  - `🛸` **Mega-Wide Trampoline + Rainbow Floor Shield (`🌈`)**: Expands the cloud paddle and restores the bottom elastic rainbow safety net!
- **8 Themed Smiling Critter Worlds**:
  1. ☀️ **DogDay's Sunny Brick Meadow** (`3` Critter Cages)
  2. 🌙 **CatNap's Starlight Crystal Cave** (`3` Critter Cages)
  3. 🎨 **CraftyCorn's Rainbow Art Studio** (`4` Critter Cages + Armored Bricks)
  4. ⚡ **Hoppy's Emerald Pinball Bounce** (`4` Critter Cages + `🔔` Bell Bumpers)
  5. 🍎 **PickyPiggy's Sweet Berry Castle** (`5` Critter Cages + `🔔` Bell Bumpers)
  6. ❄️ **Bubba's Frosty Swaying Glacier** (`5` Critter Cages + Moving Bricks + `🍄` Bumper)
  7. 💖 **Bobby's Ruby Heart Fortress** (`6` Critter Cages + Moving Bricks + `🍄` Bumper)
  8. 🔥 **KickinChicken's Golden Star Finale** (`6` Critter Cages + Full Pinball & Moving Bricks)
